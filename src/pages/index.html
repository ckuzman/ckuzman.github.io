We take the difficult ones.

Films about hard subjects and hard places, for organizations that can’t afford to get it wrong.

The projects people get nervous about: the ones that usually die in the PR meeting, and the places most crews won’t take a camera.

One of these is enough. Most of our projects are one or the other. Some are both.

  • Hard to say out loud.

    Controversial, morally complicated, emotionally heavy, or a bigger swing than the brand has taken before. The project everyone wants and legal is nervous about. We make it, and we make it hold up, because we’ve done the homework before we pitch it.

  • Hard to get to.

    Remote, uncontrolled, high-risk, or simply unwelcoming. Built to work without a base camp. Hundreds of drone flights in places most pilots won’t take: live industrial sites, tight quarters, festival grounds, third world countries, you name it.

What we do

  • Story.

    We figure out what to film before anyone touches a camera. If the easy version won’t do the job, we’ll say so before you spend money on it.

  • Production.

    Interviews, field shoots, multi-camera events, drones, cable cam. Remote locations and crowds are normal days for us. Teams with their own editors can book the day and take the raws.

  • Editing.

    Long and short form. We cut for the person who has to sit through it, not for ourselves.

  • Long form, on purpose.

    A film someone watches for twenty minutes does more for your credibility than one they scroll past in two seconds. We make the long version first and cut the short ones from it.

What we believe

A lazy video is worse than none. The safe idea costs the same to make, and nobody remembers it.

Trying to offend no one is how you convince no one. Conflict makes it a story. Vulnerability gets you trust.

The crew is careful so the film can be bold. Small enough to get in, quiet enough that people forget the camera, prepared enough that the only risk left is on screen.

Brand risk is something we manage, not something we avoid. If the easy version won’t work, we say so, even when it’s your plan.

Some stories are hard to reach. Some are hard to tell. We’re built for both, together or apart.

When anything can be generated, the real thing is worth more. We film real people in real places.

Homework is most of the job. We know the subject, the people, and the place before the camera comes out, and we plan for the plan to fail. Then we finish: on time, on budget, no surprises.
